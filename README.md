## 🚀 Projects

* 📱 **[Actua](https://actua.azimulkabir.com)**: native Android client for Actual Budget, with on-device budgeting, encrypted backups and sync. Kotlin, Jetpack Compose. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.azimulkabir.actua) · [Source](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/actua) · GPLv3
* 🎵 **[Harmony](https://harmony.azimulkabir.com)**: self-hosted music manager for Navidrome that syncs Spotify and YouTube Music playlists, tags tracks and organises the library. Docker. [Source](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/harmony)
* 🛡️ **[Sotorko (সতর্ক)](https://sotorko.azimulkabir.com)**: Android app that helps people in Bangladesh spot bKash and Nagad scams before they lose money. Bangla by default, English too. [Website source](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/sotorko-website)
* 🧾 **[Kor (কর)](https://kor.azimulkabir.com)**: offline Android app for Bangladesh personal income tax. See what you owe as the year goes on and get your wealth statement ready for the e-return. In development.

**More open source:** 📸 [Photo Migrator](https://github.com/azimul-kabir/photo-migrator), a safe merger for overlapping photo backups on Synology and macOS · ✨ Daily Creative Spark, printable creative-writing worksheets for young writers.

**Private:** 📊 Portfolio Pulse, an offline credit-portfolio dashboard for banking MIS · 📦 Inventory Management, a self-hosted trading back office.

**Contributions:** 💰 [Actuali](https://github.com/MattFaz/actuali), the iOS companion for Actual Budget.
